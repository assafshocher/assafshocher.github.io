About the motion. These animations are views of the supplied still images, not additional generated results. Most animations rebuild nested copies from the most detailed available equivalent regions, with small spatial blends at their boundaries. Rimrings uses a direct animation of the existing bands instead, with a blended loop transition. Resting thumbnails show the animation’s first frame. Use Show still to inspect the supplied result.

Rimrings keeps the visible boundaries between bands. Its display animation does not imply that an inverse was used during generation.
