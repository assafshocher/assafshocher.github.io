How to read the viewer

  • Each image carries a label that states how it was selected (for example "largest gain over ..." or "rank 1 by DISTS gain"); the examples are illustrations, not a representative sample. Aggregate results are in the paper.
  • "image / file same as round 1" compare the stored 8-bit image and the compressed file with those of round 1. For the HPCM float and clipped-float chains these values follow the first-file counts of Table 1.
  • NLBP: PixNerd uses its tuned setting at λ = .0018; at the other rates PixNerd and Z-Image use untuned settings (configurations: Table 17 in the paper's appendix).
  • Images fit the window. Click an image to switch to actual pixels (scroll to pan), and click again to fit.
  • Images are lossless and load on demand.
